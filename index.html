Data & providers

Data quality: real-time, delayed and end-of-day

Real-time means the last trade as it happens. Delayed usually means 15 minutes behind for US stocks on free plans. End-of-day means yesterday's close until the market shuts today. Crypto trades around the clock and FX five days a week, so "closed" means something different for each.

Stocks labels every number with its source and time, shows a chip when a chart is demo, partial or stale, and never fills a gap with sample data while you are using a live provider.

Free tiers limit how many requests you can make. The meters above count this browser's requests against each provider's published limits; detached panels reuse this window's data and cost nothing.

Detached displays

Put a panel in its own window or a Picture-in-Picture tile — a second monitor, a TV, or a strip docked over other apps. Panels receive prices from this window and never fetch on their own, so an extra panel costs no API credits.

Panels open one at a time: a browser grants only the first new window per click, so there is no one-click restore of a saved layout.
